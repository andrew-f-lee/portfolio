Real data · Product · Review research · PRD

The question

Where do Acorns Invest users lose clarity in the savings journey? Which feature should the product team test first?

Key findings

  • 100 public reviews across three sources, with no primary interviews.
  • 26 reviews mention transfers or automation, including 9 rated one to three stars.
  • 13/15 exact tag-set matches in the self-consistency check.

Method

  1. Collected dated reviews and reviewed current help and subscription documentation.
  2. Tagged one or two themes per review and checked a shuffled subset.
  3. Prioritized five options in RICE and designed four transfer states.
