Costco | Gothenburg market entry

Pilot paid demand before committing to a third Swedish warehouse.

Public-company analysis · Strategy · Excel · Market sizing

The question

Should Costco enter Gothenburg with a warehouse by 2029? What member and site economics would justify commitment?

Key findings

  • SEK 38.9B in modeled serviceable retail spending.
  • SEK -423M base five-year NPV.
  • 38.6% mature adoption for base five-year break-even.

Analysis preview

Method

  1. Built an issue tree around demand, competition and economics.
  2. Sized a catchment top-down and attainable sales bottom-up.
  3. Reran three scenarios and solved for the adoption threshold.
