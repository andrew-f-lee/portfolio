Olist | E-commerce repeat purchase

Late orders scored 2.27 stars versus 4.29 on time. The repeat-purchase gap was inconclusive.

Real data · SQLite · Python · Cohorts

The question

What patterns explain observed one-time purchasing on Olist? Are first-order delays associated with fewer repeat purchases?

Key findings

  • 3.04% observed repeat share across qualifying customers.
  • 2.02% versus 2.32% repeat with 90 days of follow-up.
  • 2.27 versus 4.29 mean review stars for late and on-time deliveries.

Method

  1. Joined customers and orders by order-specific ID, then grouped by stable customer ID.
  2. Audited order data and ran eight SQL queries.
  3. Compared equal-window outcomes in SQL and pandas, then adjusted for month and state in a logistic model.

Assumptions and limits

  • Historical data cover purchases observed through the platform.
  • First-order eligibility and complete follow-up reduce the analysis sample.
  • The confidence interval includes zero. Rating and repeat associations are observational.
