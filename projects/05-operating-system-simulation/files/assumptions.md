# Relay simulation assumptions

- Simulation. All names, dates, vendor quotes, performance and financial inputs are fictional. The facts sheet JSON controls numerical assumptions and the consistency check documents ties.
- Opening headcount is 80 before the planned hire. Opening ARR is $12.0 million. Runway is starting cash divided by modeled monthly burn.
- The quarter is 2027-01-04 to 2027-03-26. Launch 2027-03-04 and office 2027-03-17 are retained in all status scenarios. The revised hire start is 2027-02-22.
- Forecast bridge: $342k original office forecast, $32k gross change, $22k scope reduction, $352k revised, $8k headroom.
- No actual company records, employees, facility plans or vendor prices were used. The floor plan is illustrative. The 90-day roadmap can run beyond the specified operating quarter.
