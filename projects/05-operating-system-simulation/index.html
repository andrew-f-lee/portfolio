The question

How can a fictional Series B company manage a product release and a new office in one quarter? What happens when staffing, a vendor and the budget each slip?

Key findings

  • 40 tasks across launch, workplace and operations.
  • $352k latest office forecast within a $360k cap.
  • Three status scenarios tie to the same milestones and operating figures.

Full Gantt chart · Dependency map · Floor plan

Method

  1. Fixed the company facts and hypothetical dates before planning.
  2. Sequenced work, assigned owners and modeled three disruption responses.
  3. Checked the workbook and documents against the same budget, headcount and milestone history.
